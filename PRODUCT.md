# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Audiophiles and music enthusiasts on macOS who store high-quality audio libraries (FLAC, ALAC) on local or external storage. They value a distraction-free, premium, and native desktop listening experience.

## Product Purpose
A lightweight, high-performance native macOS music player specializing in local FLAC/ALAC playback and dynamic synced lyrics. It solves the need for a beautiful, distraction-free player that can access external drives without index lag or high memory footprint.

## Brand Personality
- Minimalist
- Immersive
- Precision-engineered
- Dark, clean, and focus-driven (monochromatic)

## Anti-references
- Cluttered streaming platforms with algorithms, banners, or social feeds (e.g., Spotify's default home screen).
- Bland, default system UI media utilities that lack emotional design.

## Design Principles
- **Aesthetic Immersion**: Let the music and typography take center stage. When playing, the artwork and synced lyrics should feel alive and visually integrated.
- **Mac Native Integration**: Look and feel like a native macOS app, matching system blurs, margins, window control layouts, and traffic lights.
- **Resource Stewardship**: Remain lightweight and fast, loading audio directly from external hard drives without stalling or memory bloat.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Large, high-contrast typography for scrolling lyrics.
- Keyboard-only navigation support and compatibility with macOS voice control/Screen Reader services.
